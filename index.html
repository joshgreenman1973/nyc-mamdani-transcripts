Everything said at Mayor Zohran Mamdani’s official events — speeches, press conferences, prepared remarks, statements, and his quotes inside press releases — plus the press releases of New York City’s agencies, all catalogued since he took office on January 1, 2026. The mayor’s words stay the center of it; the agency releases widen it into a citywide search. Look for a word or topic, and filter by agency to see who said it.

About this archive & its sources

Transcripts include reporters’ questions and other officials at the same event. Toggle Only the mayor’s words below to narrow to lines spoken or quoted by him. Most items are official transcripts the mayor’s office publishes; the archive also pulls in outside transcripts of his interviews (NPR, WNYC’s “Ask the Mayor,” C-SPAN). It also includes the NYPD’s monthly crime-statistics briefings — published by the department as press releases, not verbatim transcripts, and clearly labeled as such — so Commissioner Tisch’s crime numbers are here even when the mayor wasn’t at the podium. Each item is labeled by source and how reliable its transcript is — filter by reliability below.
